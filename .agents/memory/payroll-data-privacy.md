---
name: Payroll data privacy
description: Privacy boundary for worker names, hours, and wage rates in this app.
---

Keep payroll data on the user's device by default. Do not add server-side storage or sharing unless the user asks for it.

**Why:** Employee names, hours, and rates are sensitive, and the user did not ask for accounts or cross-device access.

**How to apply:** Preserve browser-only import, calculation, and persistence. Ask before introducing remote storage, accounts, or synchronization.
